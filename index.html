Conventional vs DFA-Based Control

The two approaches were compared on the same design requirements — coin handling, coffee selection, balance tracking, and invalid-input rejection — to see where the DFA-based design actually helps in practice rather than just on paper.

AspectConventional (if/else)DFA-Based Design
Design basisScattered if/else checks on running balanceExplicit state + transition table
DeterminismDepends on implementation disciplineGuaranteed by construction
VerifiabilityHard to check every coin/button sequenceEvery (state, input) pair is enumerable
Extending menu / pricesMay require re-wiring conditionalsOnly the BUY comparison changes
DebuggabilityBehaviour inferred from symptomsCurrent state is always known exactly
